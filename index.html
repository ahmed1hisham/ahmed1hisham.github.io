LifeRight - Medical Records Management

LifeRight is the best way to never lose a medical document ever again. You can also share your medical profile anywhere in the world!

Features

- Medical Directory: LifeRight acts as a personal medical documents directory where you can scan or upload any of you medical documents all at one place, forming an integrated, complete Medical Profile.
- Profile Sharing: A user can smoothly share their medical profile with a doctor or a hospital, and they have the ability revoke that access at anytime. A user will always be notified if someone views their Medical Profile.
- Emergency & SOS: The app provides a way to request an ICU unit at the nearest hospital during emergency, as well as an SOS option that automatically informs needed entities.
